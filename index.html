Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers before you request your audit.

What exactly is a workflow audit?
A workflow audit is a personalized review of how your POD business currently handles orders, design approvals, and fulfillment. We identify where things are slowing you down and show you how to fix them.
Is this a software or a service?
It’s both. We provide a system tailored to your workflow and help you implement it so your operations run smoothly without confusion.
Do I need technical knowledge to use this?
No. The system is designed to be simple and easy to use for you and your team—no technical experience required.
Will this replace my current tools (Shopify, WhatsApp, etc.)?
Not necessarily. We organize and streamline your workflow so your existing tools work better together—without the chaos.
How long does the audit take?
The audit is quick and focused. You’ll get clear insights into your workflow and practical recommendations without wasting your time.
Is the audit really free?
Yes, it’s completely free. We offer a limited number of audits each week to ensure quality and personalized attention.
What happens after the audit?
You’ll receive clear insights into what’s broken in your workflow and how to fix it. If you want, we can help you implement the system—but there’s no obligation.
Will this actually help me handle more orders?
Yes. By organizing your workflow and reducing manual work, you’ll be able to manage more orders without increasing stress or errors.
